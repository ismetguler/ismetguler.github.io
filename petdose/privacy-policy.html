Privacy Policy for PetDose

Last updated: July 3, 2026

PetDose ("the App") is a medication and health tracker for pets. This policy explains what information the App handles and how it is used.

Data stored on your device

Pet profiles, medication schedules, doses, and weight history that you enter into PetDose are stored locally on your device using an on-device database. This data is not uploaded to, or stored on, any server operated by us. We do not have access to it.

Notifications

Medication reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No reminder or schedule data is sent to us or any third party.

Subscriptions and purchases

PetDose offers optional premium subscriptions through Apple's In-App Purchase system, processed via RevenueCat. To manage purchases and entitlements, RevenueCat may collect an anonymous device/user identifier and purchase transaction data (e.g., product purchased, purchase date, subscription status). This information is used solely to verify and manage your subscription. See RevenueCat's privacy policy for details: revenuecat.com/privacy.

Data we do not collect

PetDose does not require an account, does not collect your name, email, or location, and does not use the data you enter for advertising or analytics purposes.

Data deletion

You can delete all app data at any time by deleting pets/medications within the App, or by uninstalling the App, which removes all locally stored data from your device.

Children's privacy

PetDose does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
